# Name and logo policy

The **code** of Intelia Launcher is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The **name and identity** are
not: Apache-2.0 section 6 grants no right to use the licensor's trade names, trademarks or logos.

**Covered:** "Intelia", "Intelia Launcher", the Intelia logo and icon, and the product names LENS, ARC, PULSE and FLOW
when used to name or market a launcher or a fork of this one.

**Allowed without asking:** saying truthfully that your work is "based on Intelia Launcher" or "a fork of Intelia
Launcher"; linking to this repository; reviews, articles and tutorials.

**Not allowed without written permission:** publishing a modified build under the Intelia name or logo; using them so
that people may think your build is official or endorsed; publishing under the application ID `dev.intelia.launcher`.

**Official builds** are only those attached to [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ondrejmacko-it/Intelia-Launcher/releases)
of this repository and signed with the project's release key (certificate SHA-256
`5d:db:08:b2:0d:7f:3e:b0:2c:0a:e2:6a:7d:8b:6a:75:fb:28:4f:e9:76:16:f4:22:60:de:d2:4c:d6:dd:99:d3`). Android refuses to
update an official install with a build signed by anyone else.

Requests: open an issue in this repository.
