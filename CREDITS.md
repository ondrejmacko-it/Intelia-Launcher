# Credits and inspiration

Intelia Launcher is written from scratch. **No source code, icons, images or other assets were copied** from the
products below. They are listed for transparency: they shaped ideas about *what* a launcher can do, and Intelia
implements its own answer. Ideas and functional principles are not protected by copyright; names below are
trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify them. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

| Product | Owner | What inspired us (functionally) |
|---|---|---|
| Kvaesitso | MM2-0 / open-source project | A search-first launcher; Intelia is deliberately **not** a fork of it (DECISIONS D1) |
| Niagara Launcher | Peter Huber | Minimal one-hand home with a single list |
| Nothing OS / Nothing Launcher | Nothing Technology Ltd. | Calm monochrome aesthetics, the test device (Phone (3)) |
| KEA Essential Remapper | Techiboy Studios | Organising key and gesture bindings with counters (`docs/KEA_PARITY.md`) |
| MT Capsule / Material Capsule-style apps | their developers | A capsule around the camera cut-out (PULSE) |
| Apple Dynamic Island | Apple Inc. | The general idea of a live area around the camera |
| Material You | Google LLC | Colours derived from the wallpaper (planned) |
| Shizuku | RikkaApps | Planned optional provider for privileged actions (not bundled; MASTER_PLAN §6) |
